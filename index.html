Launch Services (Rockets)

We provide end-to-end launch solutions for commercial and government payloads. Our "Harsha" series of launch vehicles are designed for reliability, reusability, and rapid deployment.

Harsha-L (Light Lift)

Ideal for small satellites and constellation deployment to LEO. Payload capacity up to 500kg.

Harsha-H Aces First Reusability Test

Rockets | 28 Oct 2025

Today marks a monumental step for The ChitraHarshaVPK Ventures and the 'Made in India' space program. At 09:30 IST, our flagship Harsha-H heavy-lift vehicle successfully launched a 10-tonne dummy payload to a target orbit.

But the primary mission was just beginning. The first stage booster, powered by nine of our proprietary 'Vikrant' methalox engines, performed a flawless boost-back burn and atmospheric re-entry, touching down precisely on our autonomous droneship, 'Atal'.

This achievement validates our AI-driven flight control and rapid-reusability architecture, paving the way for a dramatic reduction in launch costs for our commercial clients. Our quantum-secured network provided real-time telemetry throughout the entire flight, encrypted and managed via our internal blockchain.
